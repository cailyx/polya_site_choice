# polya_site_choice

this will be to study intuitively the framework for alternative polyA site (PAS) choice, surely a function of PAS strength, rate of transcription, and distance between alternative PASs, discussed in "The polyadenylation code: a unified model for the regulation of mRNA alternative poly adenylation" by davis and shi 2014

A polyA signal (PAS) is an RNA sequence that recruits protein machinery (the "3' processing complex") to cleave/process a pre-mRNA from a transcribing RNA pol II. In ~70% of eukaryotic genes, multiple PASs are lined up at different locations, giving rise to different possible transcripts (termed "alternative polyadenylation (APA)" events) with potentially different functions. Though many factors may enter into why one PAS is selected over another, a helpful bare-bones framework for explaining PAS selection (let's consider just 2, "PAS1" and "PAS2") is to study it as a function of PAS strengths, 3' processing factor concentrations and affinities, rate of RNA pol II transcription, and distance between PAS1 and PAS2-- the paper above provides the relevant rationale and discussion of experimental work. Briefly, we might say PAS1 will be used if it takes less time to transcribe and process PAS1 (call this time "Tp1") than to transcribe the nt between PAS1 and PAS2 ("Tt") and transcribe and process PAS2 ("Tp2"), and provided there are no factors otherwise blocking PAS1. Otherwise, PAS2 wins, as the opposite is the case-- Tp1 > Tt+Tp2. The purpose of this simulator is to explore this intuitively and help articulate questions (e.g., to what extent can a super-strong PAS1 override super-high []'s of a protein that biases PAS2?). Of course, the core variables here are all tunable in principle by the cell, on top of which other regulators like RNA-binding proteins may provide further control.


This was made mostly using Claude (05-16-26) with minor improvements by me. Note the scales and relative "weights" of each factor are currently pretty arbitrary-- please feel free to share or implement ideas for improvements (eg fixing scales, variables to add/remove, etc.)! -Cailyx
